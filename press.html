About the Game

Launch the ball, shatter bricks and trigger chain explosions across 6 space worlds and 48 hand-made levels: Deep Space, Pink Nebula, Frozen Planet, Milky Way, Emerald Nebula and the Red Giant. Every world ends with a boss that splits in two and enrages.

Explosive, armored, steel and regenerating bricks; fireball, laser and multi-ball power-ups; a FEVER mode that sets the screen on fire; and a Paddle Workshop with 6 upgrades and 4 paddle bodies. Swipe or tilt the device to steer, in portrait or landscape. Fully playable offline, no account needed.

Made by One Person

Every sound effect was synthesized from scratch and tuned for iPhone speakers, every planet and nebula texture was generated procedurally, and the haptics were built with Core Haptics so each brick type feels different under your thumb.
